100% Local On-Device Privacy Policy & FAQs
MusiAmp requires zero login and never uploads your audio files, folder names, or playlists to any external server. Local files are read strictly in-memory on your device via browser Object URLs.
Once loaded, MusiAmp plays MP3, WAV, M4A, and OGG files completely offline without Wi-Fi or mobile data, processed in real time through our 5-Band Web Audio Equalizer and limiter.
The gentle background rings run smoothly at a lightweight 20 FPS and automatically pause when your screen turns off. You can also toggle the FFT visualizer bars off anytime inside the Equalizer tab.
